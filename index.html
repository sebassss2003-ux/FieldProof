WEEK 13 · REAL HUMANS

Evidence before assumptions

Turn real user sessions into better decisions.

FieldProof is a lightweight evidence workspace designed to help early-stage teams observe real users, document friction, rank critical problems, and track improvements.

Five sessions. One evidence-based decision.

The goal is not to collect compliments. It is to understand what people actually do, where they struggle, and what the team needs to fix first.
