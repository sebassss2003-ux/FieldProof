01 · The problem

Good product decisions need more than scattered notes.

Small teams often collect useful feedback but struggle to connect what users actually do with the changes they make.

The current workaround

Spreadsheets, shared documents, screenshots and messaging apps can hold research notes. But the path from session to finding, priority, correction and retest can become fragmented. This is a problem hypothesis FieldProof is designed to investigate—not a proven claim about every team.

The FieldProof promise

Organize real sessions, preserve observations, prioritize supported friction and track whether a correction improves the same task. Keep facts, interpretations and unknowns separate.

First audience

07 · The creative method

How an idea becomes useful content.

AI can support each stage, but a human checks the evidence and approves publication.

02 · Writing

04 · Claim checking

Verify claims against their sources. Label illustrative or simulated material.

05 · Style review

Remove jargon, exaggerated promises and unclear language.

06 · Human revision

A human reviews, corrects and approves every asset before publication.

Human approval is mandatory: AI may help draft and review content, but it cannot invent participants, testimonials, research findings, campaign engagement or business results.
08 · The shadow clause

Research tools must not create false confidence.

FieldProof must never make a team believe that a guess, AI-generated summary or demo record is genuine user evidence.

  • Separate observed behavior from interpretation.
  • Label demo and synthetic data clearly.
  • Use participant role labels instead of unnecessary personal details.
  • Obtain consent before recording or collecting evidence.
  • Protect downloaded records and never expose private information.
