Track what changed after identifying a usability problem. Compare the original task with the same task after the correction to understand whether the change made a difference.

A change is not proof of improvement

A redesigned screen or proposed solution does not demonstrate success by itself. Retest the same task with users and document what actually happened before claiming that the correction worked.

If no retest has taken place, leave the result pending.

Only select an improvement, unchanged or worsened outcome when a real retest supports that conclusion.

Research integrity: Do not claim that a correction improved the product without evidence from a retest. Synthetic persona feedback can help identify clarity issues, but it is not a substitute for a real participant retest. Do not enter personal or sensitive information.
