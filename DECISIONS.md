
# FIELDPROOF — DECISIONS LOG
## Week 15 — Distribution

### 1. Venture continuity
Decision: Continue developing FieldProof.
Reason: The existing product already provides a research workflow. A new venture is unnecessary.

### 2. Positioning
Decision: Position FieldProof around evidence traceability and research integrity.
Reason: The product should help teams connect real user behavior with product decisions.

### 3. First audience
Decision: Focus on Mexican founders, product managers, UX/UI designers and small digital agencies.
Reason: These teams may need a lightweight research workflow without enterprise infrastructure.

### 4. Product scope
Decision: Keep the four existing modules:
- Recruitment and Sessions
- Evidence and Observation
- Pain Ranking and Prioritization
- Impact Tracking and Validation

Reason: Preserve the existing product and improve its presentation.

### 5. Brand identity
Decision: Use navy, blue, teal and pale blue with a clean visual system.
Main message: Evidence before assumptions.

### 6. Distribution
Decision: Create three content series and three finished assets in different formats.
Reason: Demonstrate the product's value through useful educational content.

### 7. Technology
Decision: Maintain GitHub Pages and the existing static HTML, CSS and JavaScript implementation.
Reason: Avoid unnecessary technical complexity during this sprint.

### 8. Research integrity
Decision: Separate real observations, interpretations and simulated information.
Reason: Prevent false confidence and protect research participants.

### 9. Human approval
Decision: Require human verification before publishing AI-assisted content.
Reason: AI must not invent evidence, participant quotes or results.

### 10. Next actions
- Complete the Week 15 website update.
- Verify all existing product modules.
- Complete mechanical and persona testing.
- Share two content assets with the intended audience.
- Record actual distribution results.
- Verify deployment history.

- ## Week 15 — Distribution Decision

**Evidence:** The first distribution test reached 3 people, generated 3 responses, and received 2 website clicks. Feedback was positive: people found the project interesting, understood its purpose, and considered the instructions clear.

**Decision:** Keep the current messaging and visual direction because the initial feedback suggests they communicate FieldProof effectively.

**Limitation:** The sample size is small and does not yet demonstrate market acceptance or actual product adoption.

**Next action:** Expand outreach to more potential users and collect more specific feedback about the product's usefulness and features.
